STEW-MAP (Stewardship Mapping & Assessment Project)

STEW-MAP is a collaborative stewardship mapping project designed to help communities identify, analyze, and understand organizations working to improve and protect local environmental resources.

As the project's primary technical lead, I assumed ownership of the end-to-end geospatial solution, developing the workflows, applications, documentation, and implementation resources needed to support new STEW-MAP deployments across the country.

Business Challenge

The project required a repeatable and scalable process for collecting stewardship survey responses, processing data, analyzing organizational relationships, and presenting results through modern web applications.

Stakeholders needed a solution that could be replicated in new geographic areas while remaining easy to maintain and simple enough for non-technical users to operate.

My Role

  • Served as primary developer and technical lead.
  • Owned project delivery from requirements through implementation.
  • Designed the complete workflow architecture.
  • Developed ArcGIS Online applications and supporting resources.
  • Created Python workflows for post-processing survey data.
  • Authored implementation documentation and training materials.
  • Provided technical support and guidance for future deployments.

Technical Solution

The solution connected every stage of the stewardship mapping process into a streamlined workflow.

Survey responses were collected using ArcGIS Survey123, processed through automated Python workflows, and published into ArcGIS Online for visualization and analysis.

The resulting Experience Builder application integrated survey responses, statistical information, stewardship networks, and geographic office locations into a single interactive environment that allowed stakeholders to explore and better understand stewardship activities within their communities.

Documentation & Training

One of the most significant project deliverables was a comprehensive 130-page user guide designed to help future implementations succeed without requiring extensive technical support.

The guide documented every phase of the workflow, including:

  • Survey123 data collection
  • Python notebook processing workflows
  • ArcGIS Online administration
  • Data management best practices
  • Experience Builder configuration
  • Visualization and analysis techniques

Impact

STEW-MAP transformed a complex multi-step geospatial process into a structured and repeatable solution that can be adopted by communities nationwide.

By combining survey collection, automation, visualization, and stakeholder engagement into a single integrated platform, the project improved efficiency, reduced implementation complexity, and expanded access to meaningful stewardship information.

The resulting applications and documentation continue to provide a framework for future stewardship mapping efforts, allowing organizations to better understand community partnerships and environmental initiatives.
