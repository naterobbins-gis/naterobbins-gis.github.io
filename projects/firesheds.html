Fireshed Registry Modernization

The Fireshed Registry project was developed to improve the management, analysis, and visualization of wildfire-related data used to support risk reduction, community resilience, and strategic planning efforts across the United States.

Working with stakeholders and technical teams, I helped modernize geospatial workflows, improve data management processes, automate updates, and enhance decision-support tools used by Forest Service personnel and partner organizations.

Business Challenge

The Fireshed Registry relied on data from numerous sources, making updates, analysis, and visualization difficult to manage over time. Existing workflows required significant coordination and manual effort to keep information current.

Project stakeholders needed a more sustainable approach that improved data accessibility, supported wildfire planning efforts, and provided decision-makers with reliable and up-to-date information.

My Role

  • Analyzed existing dashboard workflows and identified opportunities for improvement.
  • Consolidated and organized data from multiple enterprise sources.
  • Developed project documentation, workflow diagrams, and data management strategies.
  • Designed conceptual, logical, and physical database models.
  • Created automated FME workflows for recurring data updates.
  • Integrated GIS services with ArcGIS Experience Builder applications.
  • Presented project capabilities and outcomes to Forest Service leadership.

Technical Solution

The project focused on improving the Fireshed Registry's underlying data architecture and reducing complexity in ongoing maintenance processes.

Automated workflows were developed using FME to update key wildfire datasets, including land ownership, fuel types, wildfire history, and other supporting layers that contribute to risk analysis and planning activities.

Data services were integrated into ArcGIS Experience Builder applications and supporting dashboards to provide a more intuitive user experience while improving access to critical information.

Key Accomplishments

Dashboard Optimization

Streamlined existing dashboard workflows and improved usability for GIS professionals and decision-makers.

Database Design

Created conceptual, logical, and physical data models to support future scalability and integration.

Workflow Automation

Developed FME workflows to automate recurring updates of critical wildfire datasets.

Leadership Engagement

Demonstrated application capabilities and project value to Forest Service leadership and stakeholders.

Impact

The Fireshed Registry modernization effort improved data quality, reduced manual maintenance, and enhanced the organization's ability to analyze and visualize wildfire planning information.

By automating updates, improving data architecture, and enhancing user-facing applications, the project supported better decision-making and helped advance strategic wildfire mitigation and community resilience initiatives.

The resulting solution provided a stronger foundation for future growth while improving the efficiency and reliability of wildfire-related data management processes.

Technologies Used

ArcGIS Enterprise • ArcGIS Experience Builder • ArcGIS Dashboards • FME • Enterprise Geodatabases • Map Services • Data Modeling • Workflow Automation • Wildfire Analytics
