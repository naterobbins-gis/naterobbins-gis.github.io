Secretary's Memo 1077-004: Climate Resilience & Fireshed Analysis

This project supported the implementation of Secretary's Memo 1077-004, Climate Resilience and Carbon Stewardship on National Forests and Grasslands. The initiative focused on identifying wildfire risk, evaluating treatment opportunities, and supporting strategic planning efforts to improve landscape resilience and community protection.

Through advanced geospatial analysis, suitability modeling, enterprise data management, and modern web applications, the project helped transform complex wildfire datasets into actionable decision-support tools for Forest Service leadership.

Business Challenge

Forest Service leadership needed a way to better understand wildfire risk, identify treatment opportunities, and evaluate priorities across large geographic areas. Multiple datasets existed across organizational boundaries, but decision makers lacked an integrated platform that could support analysis, visualization, and strategic planning.

The project required combining diverse geospatial datasets, developing repeatable analytical methods, and communicating results in a way that could be easily understood by both technical and executive audiences.

My Role

  • Integrated and analyzed diverse geospatial datasets supporting wildfire planning.
  • Developed weighted raster overlay models and suitability analyses.
  • Designed data structures and enterprise publishing workflows.
  • Published map and image services to ArcGIS Enterprise and ArcGIS Online.
  • Developed ArcGIS Online Experience Builder applications and supporting StoryMaps.
  • Created user guides, data documentation, videos, and communication materials.
  • Presented analytical findings and application capabilities to Forest Service and USDA leadership.

Technical Solution

The solution integrated wildfire risk, treatment opportunity, land management, and environmental datasets into a unified analytical framework. Weighted raster overlay techniques were used to evaluate and rank suitability across landscapes, providing decision makers with actionable information at the Fireshed scale.

Data was organized and published through ArcGIS Enterprise and ArcGIS Online, allowing stakeholders to access information through modern web applications built with Experience Builder.

Additional deliverables included StoryMaps, data summaries, documentation, demo videos, and presentation materials that helped communicate project objectives and analytical results.

Impact

The project improved the Forest Service's ability to evaluate wildfire risk, identify treatment opportunities, and support climate resilience initiatives through data-driven decision making.

By combining advanced geospatial analysis, enterprise data management, modern visualization tools, and executive-level communication, the solution provided leadership with a more comprehensive understanding of conditions across the National Forest System.

The resulting workflows, applications, and analytical products created a foundation for future wildfire planning and resilience efforts while improving access to critical information throughout the organization.
