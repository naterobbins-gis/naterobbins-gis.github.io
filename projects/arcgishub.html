Forest Service ArcGIS Hub

The Forest Service ArcGIS Hub serves as the primary portal for discovering, accessing, and downloading enterprise geospatial content published by the agency. The platform provides both public and internal users with a centralized location to access authoritative datasets, maps, applications, and geospatial services.

I played a key role in the continued enhancement and management of the Hub platform, helping improve data accessibility, metadata quality, security, and user adoption while supporting integration with federal open data initiatives.

Business Challenge

Forest Service geospatial data originates from numerous program areas and enterprise systems. Users often struggled to discover available datasets, understand their purpose, or determine which resources were authoritative.

The organization needed a centralized platform that could improve data discovery, simplify access to datasets and services, maintain metadata quality, and support both public transparency and internal collaboration.

My Role

  • Led ongoing development and enhancement of the Forest Service ArcGIS Hub platform.
  • Managed integration of enterprise datasets, maps, and services.
  • Delivered training sessions, demonstrations, and national presentations.
  • Implemented access controls for internal and external users.
  • Supported Data.gov integration and metadata compliance initiatives.
  • Troubleshot content publishing, validation, and discovery issues.
  • Provided technical guidance to GIS practitioners across the agency.

Technical Solution

The ArcGIS Hub platform serves as a centralized gateway that aggregates data published through ArcGIS Online and other enterprise GIS systems. The site provides searchable access to datasets, web services, maps, and applications while promoting data consistency and discoverability.

Security configurations were implemented to ensure sensitive enterprise content remained protected behind agency firewalls, with access limited to Forest Service employees and authorized partners through ArcGIS Online authentication.

Data.gov integration workflows were maintained to ensure that publicly accessible datasets met federal metadata standards and remained discoverable through national open data initiatives.

Training & Outreach

A significant part of this effort involved promoting ArcGIS Hub adoption throughout the organization. I developed and delivered presentations on Forest Service national calls and conducted demonstrations for other USDA agencies to showcase the platform's capabilities.

These sessions helped users understand how to discover data, access services, utilize metadata, and leverage ArcGIS Hub as a critical resource for geospatial information sharing.

Impact

The Forest Service ArcGIS Hub became a trusted gateway for enterprise geospatial information, improving access to authoritative datasets and increasing transparency for both internal users and the public.

Through improved metadata quality, Data.gov integration, enhanced security controls, and user training, the platform streamlined access to geospatial resources while promoting collaboration across program areas and agencies.

These efforts helped maximize the value of enterprise GIS investments by connecting users with the data and services needed to support planning, analysis, decision making, and public engagement.
