Interagency Imagery Publishing Platform (IIPP)

The Interagency Imagery Publishing Platform (IIPP) was developed to modernize how federal agencies manage, publish, and share raster imagery and remote sensing products. The platform leverages cloud technologies and ArcGIS Enterprise capabilities to provide scalable, centralized access to imagery resources across multiple agencies.

As part of the implementation team, I supported platform deployment, imagery migration, workflow automation, service publishing, and quality assurance efforts that helped bring critical geospatial imagery resources into a modern cloud-based environment.

Business Challenge

Federal agencies manage large volumes of imagery, lidar, terrain, and remote sensing data that must remain accessible, authoritative, and easy to publish. Traditional publishing approaches often required manual processes, duplicated effort, and significant infrastructure maintenance.

The Interagency Imagery Publishing Platform was designed to streamline imagery publication, improve scalability, and enable multiple agencies to efficiently share and distribute raster data through a common cloud-based solution.

My Role

  • Supported deployment and testing of the Interagency Imagery Publishing Platform.
  • Configured Amazon Workspaces to provide secure access to the platform.
  • Integrated remote sensing, terrain, lidar, and raster thematic datasets.
  • Published imagery as ArcGIS image services.
  • Developed Python automation workflows for batch data conversion.
  • Performed platform testing, validation, and quality assurance.
  • Reviewed and improved documentation supporting future adoption.
  • Published authoritative imagery resources to ArcGIS Online and enterprise portals.

Technical Solution

The solution leveraged AWS cloud infrastructure, Amazon Workspaces, ArcGIS Enterprise, and ArcGIS Online to create a scalable imagery publishing environment capable of supporting multiple federal agencies.

Automation workflows were developed to convert raster datasets from TIFF and Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF formats into cloud-ready imagery products suitable for publication and enterprise distribution.

Published image services were registered and shared through Forest Service Enterprise Content and made available through enterprise portals and ArcGIS Hub experiences, improving discoverability and accessibility of authoritative data.

Impact

The Interagency Imagery Publishing Platform provided a more efficient and scalable approach to managing imagery resources across federal programs.

Through automation, cloud integration, and improved publishing workflows, the project reduced manual effort, increased consistency, and improved access to authoritative imagery resources used for natural resource management, planning, analysis, and decision support.

The resulting platform established a foundation for long-term imagery management while improving collaboration among agencies responsible for maintaining geospatial data resources.
