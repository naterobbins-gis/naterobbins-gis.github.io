Duke Energy Enterprise GIS Modernization

Duke Energy relies on enterprise geospatial technologies to support critical business operations, infrastructure management, asset tracking, planning, and decision-making activities across a large utility environment.

As part of an enterprise modernization initiative, I supported the deployment, configuration, administration, and upgrade of ArcGIS Enterprise and FME platforms operating across hybrid Linux and Windows environments. The project focused on improving reliability, maintainability, and scalability while positioning the platform for future growth.

Business Challenge

Enterprise GIS systems support a wide range of business functions and require continuous maintenance, upgrades, and modernization to remain secure, performant, and reliable.

Duke Energy required a modernized enterprise GIS environment capable of supporting ArcGIS Enterprise applications, service publishing, enterprise data integration, and automated ETL workflows while maintaining operational continuity across production systems.

My Role

  • Installed and configured ArcGIS Enterprise components in Linux environments.
  • Administered Portal for ArcGIS, ArcGIS Server, and ArcGIS Data Store.
  • Performed ArcGIS Enterprise, ArcGIS Server, and ArcGIS Pro upgrades.
  • Installed, configured, and upgraded FME Form and FME Flow.
  • Collaborated with GIS architects, infrastructure teams, and system administrators.
  • Supported troubleshooting, validation, testing, and deployment activities.
  • Developed documentation and repeatable deployment procedures.

Technical Solution

The modernization effort focused on establishing and maintaining a robust enterprise GIS architecture spanning Linux and Windows environments.

ArcGIS Enterprise components including Portal for ArcGIS, ArcGIS Server, and ArcGIS Data Store were installed, configured, federated, and validated to support enterprise GIS operations.

In parallel, FME Form and FME Flow environments were upgraded and optimized to support automated data transformation workflows and seamless integration with ArcGIS Enterprise services.

Key Accomplishments

Enterprise Deployment

Installed and configured ArcGIS Enterprise in secure Linux environments supporting enterprise GIS operations.

Platform Upgrades

Executed ArcGIS Enterprise, ArcGIS Server, ArcGIS Pro, and FME upgrades while maintaining stability and minimizing downtime.

Hybrid Architecture

Supported interoperability across Linux and Windows infrastructure environments.

Workflow Automation

Enhanced automated ETL processes through FME Form and Flow administration and integration.

Enterprise Platform Administration

A major focus of the project involved maintaining a healthy and scalable ArcGIS Enterprise environment by applying best practices for system configuration, service management, federation, monitoring, and performance tuning.

Activities included environment validation, service testing, upgrade planning, deployment support, troubleshooting, and coordination with infrastructure and security teams to ensure long-term platform reliability.

These efforts helped establish repeatable processes and documentation that simplify future maintenance and support activities.

Impact

The modernization effort strengthened the enterprise GIS platform by improving maintainability, reliability, and system performance across multiple environments.

Through upgrades, platform administration, automation enhancements, and infrastructure improvements, Duke Energy gained a more scalable foundation for delivering geospatial services and supporting future business initiatives.

The resulting environment aligns more closely with enterprise GIS best practices and supports long-term operational sustainability.
