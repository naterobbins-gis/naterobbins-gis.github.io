Newport News Shipbuilding Enterprise GIS Assessment

Newport News Shipbuilding engaged TRC to perform a comprehensive assessment of its enterprise GIS environment, with the goal of evaluating the current state of the platform and identifying opportunities for modernization, improved governance, and future scalability.

As the lead GIS consultant on the project, I conducted a comprehensive review of the organization's ArcGIS Enterprise environment, operational workflows, data management practices, and supporting infrastructure to develop a strategic roadmap for future improvements.

Business Challenge

Newport News Shipbuilding relied on ArcGIS Enterprise to support a wide range of geospatial functions, applications, and business processes. Over time, the environment had grown in complexity, creating challenges related to scalability, governance, maintainability, and long-term sustainability.

Leadership needed a clear understanding of the current architecture, operational risks, and modernization opportunities before making future investments in the platform.

My Role

  • Served as lead consultant for the enterprise GIS assessment.
  • Facilitated stakeholder interviews, workshops, and technical reviews.
  • Evaluated ArcGIS Enterprise architecture and infrastructure.
  • Reviewed GIS governance, operational workflows, and publishing processes.
  • Analyzed data management practices and system integrations.
  • Identified technical risks, performance constraints, and modernization opportunities.
  • Developed the final assessment report and modernization roadmap.

Assessment Focus Areas

The review included a detailed assessment of the organization's ArcGIS Enterprise deployment, including:

  • Portal for ArcGIS configuration and governance
  • ArcGIS Server architecture and federated services
  • Enterprise geodatabases and SQL Server dependencies
  • Application inventory and integrations
  • Publishing workflows and service lifecycle management
  • Performance, scalability, and high-availability considerations
  • Security, governance, and operational processes

Technical Findings

The assessment identified opportunities to improve system scalability, reduce operational complexity, strengthen governance practices, and modernize portions of the ArcGIS Enterprise environment.

Particular attention was given to service lifecycle management, data duplication, manual processes, and long-term sustainability. Additional findings highlighted opportunities for improved monitoring, automation, resiliency, and alignment with current enterprise GIS best practices.

Recommendations also addressed migration readiness for ArcGIS Enterprise 11.x and future cloud-enabled architectures.

Key Deliverables

Governance Review

Evaluated operational processes, publishing standards, and enterprise GIS governance practices.

Impact

The assessment provided Newport News Shipbuilding leadership with a clear understanding of the current state of their GIS ecosystem and actionable recommendations for future growth.

By identifying technical risks, process inefficiencies, and modernization opportunities, the project established a roadmap for improving reliability, scalability, maintainability, and overall operational effectiveness.

The recommendations positioned the organization to make informed decisions regarding future investments in ArcGIS Enterprise, infrastructure modernization, and enterprise GIS governance.
