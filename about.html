About Me

Enterprise GIS Architect, Consultant, and Geospatial Technology Leader with more than 25 years of experience helping organizations modernize GIS systems, streamline workflows, and make better decisions through geospatial technology.

Hello, and thanks for visiting my portfolio.

I'm Nathan Robbins, a Geographic Information Systems Professional (GISP) and Enterprise GIS Architect with more than 25 years of experience working in GIS, geospatial data management, enterprise systems, and technology consulting.

What started as an interest in geography, maps, and technology grew into a career that has taken me through local government, engineering, environmental consulting, military support, federal agencies, and large enterprise organizations. Today I help organizations design, modernize, and support enterprise GIS environments and geospatial solutions that enable people to make better decisions with data.

I've always enjoyed figuring out how things work, solving complex problems, and helping people accomplish their goals. GIS turned out to be the perfect career because it combines technology, data, visualization, analysis, and problem-solving into a field that can make a meaningful impact.

Throughout my career I've had the opportunity to work on everything from field data collection and database design to ArcGIS Enterprise architecture, web application development, enterprise data publishing, automation, cloud-based workflows, and national-scale geospatial initiatives. I've always been drawn to challenging projects where technology, data, and creative problem-solving come together.

Why I Love GIS

One of the things I enjoy most about GIS is that no two days are ever the same. Sometimes I'm working with enterprise infrastructure and ArcGIS Enterprise environments. Other days I'm building web applications, developing automation workflows, troubleshooting complex issues, publishing data, creating dashboards, or helping stakeholders understand how GIS can support their mission.

GIS sits at the intersection of technology, science, geography, and people. That combination has kept me passionate about the profession for more than two decades. I genuinely enjoy learning new technologies, improving workflows, and finding practical solutions to difficult challenges.

My Approach

I've always believed that successful technology projects are about more than technology. The best solutions solve real problems, improve the user experience, are easy to maintain, and continue delivering value long after a project is completed.

Whether I'm working with GIS analysts, developers, engineers, system administrators, project managers, scientists, or executive leadership, I strive to be someone who listens carefully, understands the challenge, and helps teams identify practical and effective solutions.

Colleagues often know me as someone who can bridge the gap between technical complexity and business needs. I enjoy collaboration, sharing knowledge, mentoring others, and helping organizations get the most from their geospatial investments.

Outside of Work

When I'm not working with GIS and technology, you'll usually find me spending time with my family. They are the most important part of my life and help keep me grounded and balanced.

Living in Utah provides access to some incredible outdoor opportunities, and I enjoy hiking, running, exploring new trails, and staying active through CrossFit. My appreciation for the outdoors is probably one of the reasons GIS felt like such a natural fit for me from the beginning.

I'm also a sports fan, an avid reader, and someone who enjoys movies, gaming, and learning about new technologies. Whether I'm tackling a challenging GIS project, reading a good book, following a sporting event, exploring the mountains, or spending time with friends and family, I try to approach life with curiosity, gratitude, and a commitment to continuous growth.
