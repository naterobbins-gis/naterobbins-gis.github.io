About Me

Hello, and thanks for visiting my portfolio.

I'm Nathan Robbins, a Geographic Information Systems Professional (GISP) and Enterprise GIS Architect with more than 25 years of experience working in GIS, geospatial data management, enterprise systems, and technology consulting.

What started as an interest in geography, maps, and technology grew into a career that has taken me through local government, engineering, environmental consulting, military support, federal agencies, and large enterprise organizations. Today, I help organizations design, modernize, and support enterprise GIS environments and geospatial solutions that enable people to make better decisions with data.

Throughout my career I've had the opportunity to work on everything from field data collection and database design to ArcGIS Enterprise architecture, web application development, automation, cloud-based workflows, and national-scale geospatial initiatives. I've always been drawn to challenging projects where technology, data, and problem-solving come together.

Why I Love GIS

One of the things I enjoy most about GIS is that no two days are ever the same. Sometimes I'm working with enterprise infrastructure and servers. Other days I'm helping build web applications, troubleshooting technical issues, developing automation workflows, publishing data, or working with stakeholders to understand their goals and challenges.

GIS sits at the intersection of technology, data, science, and people, and that's what has kept me passionate about the field for more than two decades. I genuinely enjoy learning new technologies, improving processes, and helping others find solutions to difficult problems.

My Approach

I've always believed that successful technology projects are about more than the technology itself. The best solutions are the ones that solve real business problems, are easy to maintain, and make life easier for the people who use them every day.

Whether I'm working with GIS analysts, developers, engineers, system administrators, project managers, or executive leadership, I strive to be someone who listens first, understands the problem, and then helps create practical solutions that provide lasting value.

Colleagues often know me as the person who can bridge the gap between technical details and business needs. I enjoy collaborating with teams, sharing knowledge, mentoring others, and finding ways to simplify complex challenges.

Outside of Work

Whether I'm reading about technology, exploring a new trail, tackling a challenging project, or spending an evening with family and friends, I try to approach life with curiosity, gratitude, and a willingness to keep learning.
